About this surface

danmercede.online is a public working log — short-form notes, experiments, and status updates on runtime governance, execution control planes, and failure-mode analysis. It is intentionally unpolished and is not canonical: entries are working artifacts, not finished positions, professional advice, or commitments. Canonical, long-form work lives at danmercede.com.

Privacy

This is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, has no accounts or logins, and collects no personal data from visitors. Nothing you do here is recorded by the site. Standard, transient web-server and CDN request logs may be retained by the hosting provider (Vercel) for operational and security purposes under their own policies; the site itself stores nothing.

Content & reuse

Posts are working notes published as-is and may be edited, superseded, or removed without notice. They represent the author's views at the time of writing and are not advice. The RSS and Atom feeds (/feed.xml, /atom.xml) are public and intended for personal feed-reader use. Brief, attributed quotation with a link back is welcome; wholesale republication is not.
